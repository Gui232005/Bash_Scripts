MEGAMERCADO - DATABASE OVERVIEW (what is stored)

DATABASE: retail_sales  (~1.8 TB, grows ~40 GB/month)
  stores ................ store_id, name, city, format, opening_hours
  products .............. sku, name, category, unit, price, vat, supplier_id
  sales_transactions .... transaction_id, store_id, date_time, cashier_id, total, payment_method
  sales_items ........... transaction_id, sku, quantity, unit_price, discount
  returns ............... return_id, transaction_id, sku, quantity, reason
  promotions ............ promo_id, sku, start_date, end_date, discount_pct

DATABASE: retail_hr  (~25 GB)
  employees ............. employee_id, name, department, role, hire_date, contract_type
  shifts ................ employee_id, store_id, date, start_time, end_time, break_minutes
  attendance ............ employee_id, date, status (present/absent/vacation), hours_worked
  payroll ............... employee_id, month, gross, deductions, net

DATABASE: retail_stock  (~300 GB)
  inventory ............. store_id, sku, quantity, min_level, last_update
  suppliers ............. supplier_id, name, contact, payment_terms
  purchase_orders ....... order_id, supplier_id, store_id, order_date, delivery_date, status
  deliveries ............ delivery_id, order_id, received_date, quantity_received

DATABASE: retail_customers  (~600 GB)  - personal data, GDPR protected
  customers ............. customer_id, loyalty_card, name, email, city
  loyalty_points ........ customer_id, points, last_purchase
  customer_purchases .... customer_id, transaction_id

QUESTIONS THIS DATA CAN ANSWER
- Which products sold the most in each store each day?
- Who worked in each store on a given day, and for how many hours?
- Which products are running low and need to be reordered?
- What was the revenue per section, per store, per day?
